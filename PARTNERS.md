## What partners shape (and what they do not)

Partners may field **works teams**, host rounds, and supply prizes. Partners **may not** alter the Regulations, the homologation list, or any scrutineering outcome — governance and stewarding are independent (REGULATIONS Art. 10, 12). Integrity is the product; we protect it.

## The reference platform

The open **DwarfStar4** engine serves as the reference scrutineering harness (per-phase profilers + live power sampling + correctness gate). Technical Partners can help extend it into a vendor-neutral, multi-accelerator measurement standard — a public good for the whole field.
